Sausage's eggs

It is a quick and practical recipe that lets you get the most out of your weekends by giving you a good protein source with little effort and a lot of flavor 😁

Ingredients

  • eggs
  • salt
  • sausage
  • oil

Instructions

  1. The first step would be to fry the sausages until they are golden brown, not burnt.
  2. Then, in a bowl, you need to mix at least 4 eggs with salt so you can have a uniform mixture
  3. After that, without turning off the fire of the sausage, you need to pour the eggs evenly over the sausages
  4. Finally, you need to cook in low fire until the egg is golden brown, not burnt, and you and your family will be ready to enjoy
